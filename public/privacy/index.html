Privacy Policy

Last updated November 6, 2026

Kide is built by Quizbiz LLC. This page explains what we do — and mostly, what we don't do — with information.

Pip's games

Pip the Sprout and Kide's other games run in your child's browser. A child does not sign in. There are no ads shown to children. The companion they pick and listening permission stay in this browser. Play on this phone can also keep practice timing on a signed-in parent account, as described below.

A parent account

A parent account is for a grown-up. If you open one, you give us an email address. We send a 6-digit code to that address so we know it is yours. The code lasts 10 minutes. You can then save a passkey on your device, so the next sign-in is a tap. The private part of a passkey never leaves your device. We keep the email, and the public part of the passkey, so we can recognize you next time.

Sign-in is the same service the other Quizbiz sites use. You are not making a separate password here. Your child's voice stays in the browser. While you are signed in, play can also keep practice timing on that account: which item, which skill, how long the answer took, whether it was right, and whether it was ordinary play or the grown-up challenge. That is not a child's name, and it is not the only thing a parent account holds.

While you are signed in, this site keeps a short-lived sign-in cookie in the browser. If you unlock family play, this phone can also keep a device cookie so the grown-up timer stays off here. Opening a share link can set a short cookie so we know which grown-up sent it. These cookies are not used to advertise. The games do not set the sign-in cookie.

Pip's voice, and your microphone

Pip reads every question out loud. That voice is a set of recordings that ship with the game — playing them needs no microphone, and no internet connection after the first visit.

Separately, and only if a grown-up switches it on in the Parent Corner, Pip can listen so your child can say an answer instead of tapping. We deliberately offer this only on devices where the browser can recognise speech on the device itself. If your browser would have to send your child's voice to a company's servers to understand it, we don't offer the feature at all — there is no cloud option to turn on.

When listening is on: no audio is recorded, saved, or transmitted. What your child says is turned into a word inside the browser, compared against the two or three answers on screen, and discarded immediately. Nothing about it is stored, and nothing is used to recognise or identify anyone. You can switch listening off at any time in the Parent Corner, and doing so erases the permission we stored.

What we do collect

If a parent chooses to enter an email address — to be notified about new activities, or to open a parent account — we store that email address so we can send that note or that sign-in code. A notify note can also keep a short name and why they wrote, if they typed those in.

If that parent is signed in, we can also store the practice timing described above, and a family-play unlock flag after they pay. If they join the weekly board, we store the handle they chose and their points for that week.

On this device, the parent page can keep a practice record (which word, which sound, whether it was heard) and, if they type it, a child's age in years for the appointment card. We do not ask for a child's name on the parent account. A gift card can put a name in the link; that name stays in the link, and we do not save it on our servers. A notify note can keep a short name if someone types one. Typing a name is not the same as asking for a child's name, and it is not a promise that no name can ever be typed. We do not collect a child's date of birth, photo, location, or voice.

Children's privacy

Kide is meant to be used by children together with a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Kids never sign in. The account is the grown-up's email. It is not a record of the child.

Deleting what this device kept

Delete everything on the parent page clears the practice record stored in this browser. That button does not close the parent account, and it does not claim to wipe every server row. To close a parent account, email hello@kide.us.

No ads, no trackers

Kide does not run third-party ads, analytics, or tracking scripts on its games. To be exact about the one piece of code here that isn't ours: our hosting provider, Cloudflare, automatically adds a small script to pages it serves in order to keep bots out. It is a security measure, we receive no data from it, and it is not used to profile, track, or advertise to anyone. Beyond that, the cookies this site sets are the ones described above.
