Privacy Policy

Last updated July 27, 2026

Kide is built by Quizbiz LLC. This page explains what we do — and mostly, what we don't do — with information.

Pip's games

Pip the Sprout and Kide's other games run entirely in your child's browser. There are no accounts, no logins, and no ads shown to children. Game progress and settings are saved only in your device's local storage — we never receive them, and they never leave your device.

Pip's voice, and your microphone

Pip reads every question out loud. That voice is a set of recordings that ship with the game — playing them needs no microphone, no account, and no internet connection after the first visit.

Separately, and only if a grown-up switches it on in the Parent Corner, Pip can listen so your child can say an answer instead of tapping. We deliberately offer this only on devices where the browser can recognise speech on the device itself. If your browser would have to send your child's voice to a company's servers to understand it, we don't offer the feature at all — there is no cloud option to turn on.

When listening is on: no audio is recorded, saved, or transmitted. What your child says is turned into a word inside the browser, compared against the two or three answers on screen, and discarded immediately. Nothing about it is stored, and nothing is used to recognise or identify anyone. You can switch listening off at any time in the Parent Corner, and doing so erases the permission we stored.

What we do collect

If a parent chooses to enter an email address — on this site or inside a game's Parent Corner — to be notified about new activities, we store that email address so we can send that one notification. We don't collect names, ages, photos, locations, or any other information about your child.

Children's privacy

Kide is meant to be used by children together with a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. The only information Kide ever stores is an email address, and only when a parent or guardian chooses to provide one. We never collect a child's name, age, photo, location, or voice.

No ads, no trackers

Kide does not run third-party ads, analytics, or tracking scripts on its games. To be exact about the one piece of code here that isn't ours: our hosting provider, Cloudflare, automatically adds a small script to pages it serves in order to keep bots out. It is a security measure, we receive no data from it, and it is not used to profile, track, or advertise to anyone. Beyond that and what's strictly necessary to serve the page, this site sets no cookies.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and change the date above.
