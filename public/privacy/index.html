Privacy Policy

Last updated November 9, 2026

Kide is built by Quizbiz LLC. This page explains what we do — and mostly, what we don't do — with information.

Pip's games

Pip the Sprout and Kide's other games run entirely in your child's browser. A child does not sign in. There are no ads shown to children. Game progress and settings are saved only in your device's local storage — we never receive them, and they never leave your device.

A parent account

A parent account is optional. If you open one, you give us an email address. We send a 6-digit code to that address so we know it is yours. The code lasts 10 minutes. You can then save a passkey on your device, so the next sign-in is a tap. The private part of a passkey never leaves your device. We keep the email, and the public part of the passkey, so we can recognize you next time.

Sign-in is the same service the other Quizbiz sites use. You are not making a separate password here. Your child's game, progress, and voice stay on the device, the way they always have. The account is the grown-up's email. It is not a record of the child.

While you are signed in, this site keeps a short-lived sign-in cookie in the browser. It is not used to advertise. The games do not set it.

Pip's voice, and your microphone

Pip reads every question out loud. That voice is a set of recordings that ship with the game — playing them needs no microphone, and no internet connection after the first visit.

Separately, and only if a grown-up switches it on in the Parent Corner, Pip can listen so your child can say an answer instead of tapping. We deliberately offer this only on devices where the browser can recognise speech on the device itself. If your browser would have to send your child's voice to a company's servers to understand it, we don't offer the feature at all — there is no cloud option to turn on.

When listening is on: no audio is recorded, saved, or transmitted. What your child says is turned into a word inside the browser, compared against the two or three answers on screen, and discarded immediately. Nothing about it is stored, and nothing is used to recognise or identify anyone. You can switch listening off at any time in the Parent Corner, and doing so erases the permission we stored.

What we do collect

If a parent chooses to enter an email address — to be notified about new activities, to open a parent account, to claim a share link, or for a family play receipt — we store that email address so we can send that note, that sign-in code, or that receipt, or so the claim can be recorded. When you claim a share link, that email is sent to our billing service together with your share code. We don't collect names, ages, photos, locations, or any other information about your child.

Free play is a few minutes a day. To keep count, this site sets a cookie with a random device number and stores today's minutes against it. When a parent is signed in, today's minutes are also stored against that email. After a parent buys family play, we also store the email they gave us against that same device number, so this phone or tablet can keep playing. The device number is not your child's name. We do not use it to advertise.

If you arrived through another parent's share link, a cookie remembers that parent's share code for 30 days. The code names who shared the link, not who opened it. We send that code with family play checkout. We also send the code to our billing service, with the step name, phone, tablet, or computer when we know it, the time, and the totals of link opens, checkouts, and paid family play. That service files a referral credit on a queue for the parent who shared, after a paid purchase. The queue is a record for later. It is not a payment from this page, and we do not contact them to say you bought.

When family play is paid, we keep a marker so that purchase is counted once. The marker is a SHA-256 scramble of the receipt email and the purchase id. It cannot be turned back into the email, and it does not expire. The step count itself does not store the email.

To learn which parts of this site work, we count these steps: a share link opened; a preview card fetched by a message app before anyone taps; a tap on play from the home page; a day of free play started; the free minutes used up; the family play page opened, and whether that was from the free-minute gate, from Pip's goodnight, or opened directly; the grown-up check passed; the grown-up check missed; checkout tapped; checkout started; checkout that did not start; checkout left before payment; and family play bought. Each count is the step name, a time, and the share code when there was one. A link open also records phone, tablet, or computer. Counts that carry the same share code can be read together. A count does not include a device number, a cookie value, an IP address, or an email address, and it does not include what a child answered in a game. The free-play minute meter, which already runs during play, is what records that a day of play started and that the free minutes were used up, including the share code when the cookie is set.

On pages written for parents — the home page, sound pages, guides, this privacy page, the terms, the clinician page, and the card maker — the browser sends a note to https://riskfreetrial.org/api/analytics when the page opens, when it is scrolled halfway, and when a link or button is tapped. The note names this site, the page, a short label taken from the button's own words, where that link points, and the site of the previous page. It does not include a cookie, a device number, or a name, and nothing joins two visits. Pip's games, practice, and the family play page do not send these notes. A browser with do-not-track or global privacy control on sends none.

Children's privacy

Kide is meant to be used by children together with a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. The free-play minute count is a device number and today's minutes. When a parent is signed in, today's minutes are also kept with that email. After a parent buys family play, the email they chose is stored with that device number. Step counts use the share code of the parent who shared a link, so those counts can be grouped by that code. They do not name the child. The only other personal information Kide stores is an email address, and only when a parent or guardian chooses to provide one. We never collect a child's name, age, photo, location, or voice.

No ads

Kide does not show third-party ads. Pip's games do not send analytics. The parent-page notes above go to our own site at riskfreetrial.org, with no visitor id. To be exact about the one piece of code here that isn't ours: our hosting provider, Cloudflare, automatically adds a small script to pages it serves in order to keep bots out. It is a security measure, we receive no data from it, and it is not used to profile, track, or advertise to anyone. Beyond that, this site sets only the cookies described above: the sign-in cookie after a parent signs in, the free-play minute count, and a share code when you arrive through a share link. None of them is used to advertise. The share code, the step name, the device class, the time, and those step totals are sent to our billing service, as described above. We do not sell them.
