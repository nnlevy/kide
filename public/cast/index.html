How it works

  1. You set it up. A first name or nickname if you like, cast or boot, leg or arm, the color, and the next visit date.
  2. You hand it over. Your child meets Pip in a matching cast. Story, stickers, games and rest time are big pictures, no reading needed.
  3. Pip helps them rest. When a game ends, Pip gets sleepy and asks to be tucked in. Only a grown-up can wake Pip up again.

Tonight's story, written for your child

In the grown-up corner, tap what happened today and one thing your child loves. Pip writes a short bedtime story about the two of them, with AI. The story uses the cast set-up saved at the start (what they have, where, the color, and crutches) together with those taps, and the phone also sends this phone's device cookie. The device cookie lasts one year. Your child's name never leaves your phone for the story: it comes back with a blank, and your phone fills in the name. We keep a count of stories for the UTC day on that device number, and that count is stored for 36 hours. A new UTC day starts the allowance again. We do not keep the story or the taps. If the story writer is unavailable, Pip writes one right on the phone.

Questions parents ask

How do I explain a cast to my 3 year old?

Keep it short and say it the same way every time: "Your bone got a little crack. The cast holds it still, like a big hug, while your body works on mending it." Pip's story lets your child watch it happen to someone else first.

What can a toddler do with a cast on their leg?

Ask your child's doctor what is safe. For the sitting-down hours, every Cast Club game needs only a finger.

Is this medical advice?

No. Pip is for comfort and routine. For pain, itching, swelling, moving, bathing or when the cast comes off, ask your child's doctor.

What do you store about my child?

On this phone we keep the nickname, the cast set-up, the visit date, the stickers, which chapters were opened, tonight's story, and whether family play is already on. There is no account. The name stays on this phone unless you send the card, and that picture can include the name. A bedtime story sends the cast set-up, the taps you pick, and this phone's device cookie. The device cookie lasts one year. It never sends the name. We keep a count of stories for the UTC day on that device number, and that count is stored for 36 hours. A new UTC day starts the allowance again. Delete Cast Club on this phone clears what was saved here. It does not clear the device cookie or that story count.

Does it work for a boot, a splint, a sling or crutches?

Yes. Pip wears whatever your child has, and the story and words follow along.
