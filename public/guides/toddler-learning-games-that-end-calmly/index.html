Choosing a game

Toddler learning games that end without a meltdown

Most toddler learning games are judged on what they teach. The harder question is what happens when it's time to stop. Here is a five-point check you can run on any game in a couple of minutes, and how Pip answers each point.

1. Does it end on its own?

If the only way a session ends is you taking the tablet away, the ending will feel like a loss every time. Look for a game with a built-in, child-sized reason to stop. Pip gets sleepy as the sky darkens and asks to be tucked in, so your child says goodnight instead of losing a fight. How the goodbye works.

2. Can a child who can't read play it?

A toddler game that relies on written instructions quietly turns you into the narrator. Pip reads every question aloud, so Colors, Counting, Shapes, Letters and Feelings work for children who don't read yet. Word practice is Say it with me.

3. Is anything scored, timed or rewarded?

Pip's routines, like going potty and washing hands, are pretend play with nothing scored or timed. Why nothing is scored. The quizzes show leaves and a star recap: leaf dots on each game, a leaf bar while your child plays, and stars when Pip says goodnight. Parent Corner keeps a streak for each quiz. Challenge adds a timer, points and a streak.

4. Are there ads, and where does your child's voice go?

Check the store listing or the privacy page for ads and for what happens to anything your child says. Kide has no ads. The microphone is on only when speech is understood on the device: the browser's on-device recognizer in the garden, or the model shipped with Say it with me. When neither of those can understand speech, the microphone stays off and your child taps instead. Exactly how that works.

5. Can you try it before you sign up?

You should be able to watch a session, including how it ends, before you pay for anything. Pip's practice starts free, and kids never sign in.

The short version

  • It ends on its own, with a reason your child understands.
  • It reads aloud: Colors, Counting, Shapes, Letters and Feelings, plus Say it with me for words.
  • Potty and handwashing are not scored or timed. The quizzes show leaves and a star recap. Challenge adds a timer and points.
  • No ads. The microphone is on only when the garden's on-device recognizer or the Say it with me model can understand speech, and your child taps when it cannot.
  • You can try it, ending included, before you pay.
