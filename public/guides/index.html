Why Pip works the way it does

Every routine in Pip's world is built on somebody's actual research, not a hunch about what looks nice on a screen. These are the honest reasons — written for the parent, not the pitch deck.

Why Pip's potty routine is never scored A reward that gets withdrawn makes toddlers less helpful, not more — and about a quarter of children develop stool withholding, which pressure makes worse. Here's what we built instead. The screen time that ends without a fight Most screen-time apps end with a limit you enforce. Pip ends by getting sleepy and asking to be tucked in — so the goodbye is something your child does with you, not to them. Why we don't send your child's voice anywhere If a browser can't understand speech without sending it to a server, we simply don't turn the microphone on. No cloud fallback exists to switch on by mistake — here's exactly how that works today, and what's still being tested.
